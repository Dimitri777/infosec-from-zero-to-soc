# How to Create Mature Information Security Processes from Scratch

## Core Principle

Mature Information Security is not built by buying expensive tools first.  
It is built by understanding the business, identifying real risks, and implementing processes in the right order.

---

## Stage 1. Understand What You Are Protecting

- Identify critical assets (data, systems, business processes).
- Understand business goals and the real impact of potential incidents.
- Identify applicable regulatory requirements (e.g. personal data protection laws).

**Output:** Clear list of what matters most to the organization.

---

## Stage 2. Quick Current State Assessment

- What security controls already exist (antivirus, access management, backups, logging, policies)?
- Where are the biggest gaps?
- Who is currently responsible for security-related topics (often — nobody clearly)?

**Output:** Honest picture of the starting point.

---

## Stage 3. Establish Foundational Processes

Focus on a small number of critical processes first:

1. **Access Management**  
   Account lifecycle, privileged access, timely revocation.

2. **Endpoint & Infrastructure Protection**  
   EPP/antivirus, patching, basic network segmentation.

3. **Incident Management**  
   How incidents are detected, reported, investigated, and closed.

4. **Vulnerability Management**  
   Regular scanning and remediation of critical vulnerabilities.

5. **Personal Data / Regulatory Compliance**  
   Handling of personal data according to applicable laws.

6. **Third-Party / Vendor Risk**  
   Basic control over suppliers and contractors.

7. **Backup & Recovery**  
   Regular backups and tested recovery procedures.

---

## Stage 4. Policies and Regulations

- Create short, practical documents (not long theoretical papers).
- Information Security Policy.
- Procedures for access, incidents, personal data handling.
- Simple guidelines for employees.

---

## Stage 5. People, Tools, Metrics

- Start with existing resources + selective external help.
- Hire dedicated people when the volume of work justifies it.
- Choose tools based on real needs, not trends.
- Introduce simple metrics:
  - Time to detect / respond to incidents
  - Percentage of critical vulnerabilities closed on time
  - Coverage of systems with up-to-date protection
  - Completion rate of security awareness activities

---

## Key Principle

Information Security must reduce real business risk.  
Start with the most valuable assets and the most probable threats.  
Do not try to implement a perfect framework in the first months.
